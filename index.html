Kraftwerk: Multimedia Tour 2026

Hangar Luka Beograd, Port of Belgrade

The pioneering German electronic act brings its immersive audiovisual Multimedia Tour to the banks of the Sava for one night only — iconic sound paired with striking 3D visuals.

Moby Live at Kalemegdan

Kalemegdan Fortress, Donji Grad

Moby returns to Belgrade for the first time in 15 years, performing career-spanning classics inside the fortress walls overlooking the Sava and Danube.

6500–13900 RSD

UFC Fight Night: Medić vs. Rodriguez

Belgrade Arena, Novi Beograd

History in Serbia — the UFC's first-ever event in the country lands at Belgrade Arena, headlined by hometown welterweight Uroš Medić on a stacked 13-fight card.

Tickets via official UFC channels

Belgrade Beer Fest 2026

Ušće Park, Novi Beograd

Four days of open-air rock, food and beer at the confluence of the Sava and Danube — headlined this year by Bijelo Dugme's 50th-anniversary reunion show.

Free entry before 19:30
